# How security reports are handled

Reports that describe a possible vulnerability, exposed credentials, or unauthorized access are moved to a restricted queue reviewed by the security specialist team. Support does not discuss technical details of an unconfirmed vulnerability in the ticket thread.

## What customers receive

The customer receives an acknowledgement that the report was routed to the security team. Do not ask the customer to post secrets, tokens, or exploit details in the ticket. If a credential may be exposed, recommend rotating it.
