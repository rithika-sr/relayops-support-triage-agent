# TaskFlow Cloud 2.5

Rollout began on 3 November 2026 and proceeds by account.

## Webhooks

The webhook delivery service was rewritten for higher throughput. Signature format and the X-TaskFlow-Signature header are unchanged.

## Notifications

Digest emails are now batched per workspace.

## Projects

Archived projects are excluded from global search.
