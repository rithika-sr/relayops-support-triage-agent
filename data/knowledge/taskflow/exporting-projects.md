# Exporting projects to CSV and XLSX

Open a project, choose Export, and select CSV or XLSX. Synchronous exports download directly in the browser.

## Large projects

Projects with more than 50,000 tasks should use the asynchronous export. Choose Export, then "Email me when ready" is replaced in version 2.4 by "Prepare in background": the export appears under Settings, then Exports, and stays available for 7 days.

## Row limits

Each plan has a maximum number of rows per synchronous export. See the plan entitlements policy for the current limits.

## Custom fields

Custom fields are exported as additional columns. Fields hidden from the board view are still exported.
